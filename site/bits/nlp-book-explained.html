Natural Language Processing — Book Explained.

Plain-English companion pages for Daniel Jurafsky & James H. Martin — Speech and Language Processing, 3rd edition. These notes focus on the mid-semester NLP arc: statistical language modeling, vector meaning, neural networks for language, and sequence labeling with POS tagging, HMMs, Viterbi, and modern neural taggers.

The goal is not to replace SLP3. The goal is to make the chapter logic easier to revise: intuition first, equations second, implementation gotchas always visible.
