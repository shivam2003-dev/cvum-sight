RL references — books, courses, and the key papers.

rl references papers courses

The textbook, the canonical lecture courses, and the landmark papers worth keeping for Deep Reinforcement Learning — the mid-sem foundations and the deep-RL milestones beyond them.

Textbook (T1)

  • Sutton & Barto — Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed., MIT Press) · the course textbook; free official PDF. Ch 1–5 = the mid-sem.

Lecture courses

  • David Silver — UCL Reinforcement Learning course · the classic 10-lecture series; closely follows Sutton & Barto.
  • Berkeley CS285 — Deep Reinforcement Learning · Sergey Levine's graduate deep-RL course.
  • OpenAI Spinning Up in Deep RL · practical, code-first intro to policy-gradient & actor-critic methods.

Landmark papers

  • Mnih et al. (2013) — Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQN) · deep Q-networks; the spark of deep RL. (Cited in the MDP lecture.)
  • Mnih et al. (2015) — Human-level control through deep RL · the Nature DQN paper.
  • Mnih et al. (2016) — Asynchronous Methods for Deep RL (A3C) · asynchronous advantage actor-critic.
  • Schulman et al. (2017) — Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) · the workhorse policy-gradient algorithm.
  • Silver et al. (2016) — Mastering the game of Go (AlphaGo) · MCTS + deep RL beats a Go champion.
  • Ault et al. (2020) — Learning an Interpretable Traffic Signal Control Policy · the traffic-signal MDP example from the slides.
