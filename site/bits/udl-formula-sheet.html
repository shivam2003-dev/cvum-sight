PCA and component analysis

\[ \bar{x}=\frac1N\sum_i x_i,\qquad \Sigma=\frac1N\sum_i(x_i-\bar{x})(x_i-\bar{x})^T \]
\[ \Sigma u_j=\lambda_j u_j,\qquad h(x)=U_k^T(x-\bar{x}),\qquad \hat{x}=\bar{x}+U_kh(x) \]

Keep largest \(k\) eigenvalues/eigenvectors. Explained variance ratio: \(\lambda_j/\sum_l\lambda_l\).

Autoencoders

\[ h=f(W_ex+b),\qquad \hat{x}=g(W_dh+c) \]
\[ L_{\text{MSE}}=\lVert x-\hat{x}\rVert^2,\qquad L_{\text{BCE}}=-\sum_j[x_j\log\hat{x}_j+(1-x_j)\log(1-\hat{x}_j)] \]
\[ \theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\eta\nabla_\theta L,\qquad J=\mathbb{E}[(X-\alpha\beta X)^2]+\lambda(\alpha^2+\beta^2) \]

Convolution / transposed convolution

\[ H_{\text{conv}}=\left\lfloor\frac{H+2p-k}{s}\right\rfloor+1,\qquad H_{\text{tconv}}=(H-1)s-2p+k \]
\[ \#\text{Conv2D params}=(k_hk_wc_{in}+1)c_{out} \]

Likelihood and Laplace MLE

\[ \theta^*=\arg\max_\theta \sum_{i=1}^N\log p_\theta(x^{(i)}) \]
\[ p(x|\mu,b)=\frac{1}{2b}\exp\left(-\frac{|x-\mu|}{b}\right),\qquad \hat{\mu}=\operatorname{median}(x_i),\quad \hat{b}=\frac1N\sum_i|x_i-\hat{\mu}| \]

Autoregressive models

\[ p(x_1,\ldots,x_D)=\prod_{i=1}^D p(x_i|x_1,\ldots,x_{i-1}),\qquad \log p(x)=\sum_i\log p(x_i|x_{<i}) \]

Binary MADE samples with Bernoulli probabilities in order \(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_D\).

Flows

\[ y_a=x_a,\qquad y_b=x_b\odot \exp(s(x_a))+t(x_a),\qquad \log|\det J|=\sum_j s_j(x_a) \]
