Computational Intelligence: An Introduction — explained.

A nature-inspired route through adaptive systems: neural learning, evolutionary computation, swarm intelligence, artificial immune models, fuzzy systems, and rough sets. Every finished chapter is a detailed, story-led master's companion with a first-principles map, central derivation, worked case, failure modes, fair evaluation plan, study lab, oral-exam questions, and complete summary.

Scope: all 23 numbered chapters, aligned to the 2nd edition table of contents. Independent educational notes; they do not copy or replace the book. Open the official Wiley book page ↗
