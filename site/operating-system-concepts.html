Operating System Concepts — chapter notes.

Original companion notes for Abraham Silberschatz, Peter Baer Galvin, and Greg Gagne, Operating System Concepts, 10th edition. Each chapter is rewritten as a study article with SEO title, introduction, explanation, key concepts, examples, FAQs, takeaways, and conclusion.

These notes are not copied from the textbook. They are independent learning material organized around the book's chapter topics.
