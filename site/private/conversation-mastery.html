Course 01 · 42-day program · 6 weeks × 7 days

42-Day Conversation Mastery.

One concrete skill per day, grouped into six themed weeks. Each day is a full lesson: the idea and the principle behind it, a step-by-step how, ready-to-use scripts, a worked scenario, a starter drill and a stretch drill, common mistakes, and what "good" looks like. Do one day at a time — reading isn't practising.

How to run it: open the week page, read one day in the morning (~4 min), pick a drill, run it in a real conversation that day, and jot one line at night. One day = one rep. Don't binge — the point is 42 small reps becoming reflexes.
Week 1

Foundations

Open, listen, connect — the four moves every conversation stands on.

Days 1–7 · Icebreakers → Storytelling Week 2

Rapport & Flow

Make it warm and keep it moving — compliments, names, groups, humour.

Days 8–14 · Compliments → Humour Week 3

Days 15–21 · Safe topics → Empathy Week 4

Days 22–28 · Rapport-fast → Enthusiasm Week 5

Mastery Moves

The advanced layer — transitions, validation, advice, vulnerability, culture.

Days 29–35 · Transitions → Positivity Week 6

Character & Growth

Who you are in the room — disagreement, authenticity, confidence, growth.

Days 36–42 · Disagreements → Continuous
