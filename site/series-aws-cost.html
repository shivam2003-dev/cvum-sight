SERIES // CLOUD COST ENGINEERING · AWS · 10 OF 10 PARTS PUBLISHED

Cutting the AWS Bill
28 Case Studies.

Techniques, thinking patterns and a playbook, distilled from 28 public, numbers-backed accounts of AWS cost reduction published between 2012 and September 2026: Levels.fyi, Segment, Duolingo, Lyft, Airbnb, Canva, Snap, Rewind, Pinterest, Honeycomb, Zomato, Zendesk, Salesforce, Tinybird, Moneysmart, HERE, Amazon Prime Video, Recall.ai, DoorDash, nOps, 37signals, Dropbox, X, Prerender.io, OneUptime and GEICO. The series explains how the engineers reasoned, what they refused to trust and what they got wrong. Savings are company-reported and unaudited; treat every number as a signal of what is possible, then measure your own.
